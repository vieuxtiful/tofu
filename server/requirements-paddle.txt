## 🍢 ToFU — isolated PaddleOCR interpreter (.venv-paddle)
## vieuxtiful
##
## PaddleOCR is cicerone's CJK engine. it is NOT optional and NOT a fallback:
## EasyOCR/CRAFT fragments stacked vertical CJK signage into one box per
## character, and Paddle's DB detector + angle classifier reads those columns
## natively (measured 4x recall / 4x transcription accuracy on dense CJK
## street scenes, CP-1, scripts/eval_paddle.py).
##
## MODEL VERSION IS RESOLVED BY LANGUAGE, not pinned here. Under
## paddleocr==3.7.0, japan/ch/ch_tra/en and the Latin set already select
## PP-OCRv6_medium_det + PP-OCRv6_medium_rec; korean/arabic/cyrillic still
## select PP-OCRv5. Pass --ocr-version to eval_paddle.py to compare them.
## Paddle's published v6-over-v5 figures are measured on THEIR corpus and are
## not ToFU scene-text results.
##
## it lives in its OWN interpreter because paddlepaddle force-replaces the
## app venv's numpy/opencv on install. nothing here may be installed into
## .venv — cicerone talks to it out-of-process through
## scripts/paddle_worker.py, exchanging JSON over temp files.
##
## create:
##   py -3.13 -m venv .venv-paddle
##   .venv-paddle\Scripts\python -m pip install -r server/requirements-paddle.txt
##
## cicerone finds it via PaddleOCRBackend.is_available() (defaults to
## <repo>/.venv-paddle, override with TOFU_PADDLE_VENV). when the venv is
## absent the backend degrades to NullBackend instead of raising — so a
## missing venv looks like "no CJK recall", not a crash. check
## is_available() first when CJK detection quality regresses.
